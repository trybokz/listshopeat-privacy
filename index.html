List.Shop.Eat — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

About the app

List.Shop.Eat is a simple shopping-list application published by Red Dog Creative. It is designed to work offline without requiring a user account.

Information stored on your device

Shopping-list items and preferences are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB and localStorage. This information is used to maintain your shopping list and preferences.

Data collection and sharing

List.Shop.Eat does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share your shopping-list information with the developer or third parties. The app does not include advertising or analytics services.

Internet access

The Android application uses Capacitor and may declare internet access for its underlying framework. Shopping-list functionality does not require an online account or a developer-operated server.

Data retention and deletion

Shopping-list information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application. Device backups, if enabled, may be managed separately by your operating system.

Children's privacy

List.Shop.Eat is a general-purpose shopping utility and is not specifically designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
